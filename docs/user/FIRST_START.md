# First start

Have the assembled radio, its USB power connection, your home Wi-Fi name and password, and a phone or computer
ready. Avela Piccolo uses a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

## Power on

Connect USB power using the connection provided for your device. The Radio screen opens. The screen is operated with
the knob and Back button; tapping it does not navigate.

Turn the knob to lower the volume before connecting for the first time. The radio can start playing as soon as it
joins Wi-Fi. You can also press the knob to stop playback while setting it up.

## Connect your network

Follow [Connect for the first time](../../components/avela_network/docs/user/WIFI.md#connect-for-the-first-time).
That procedure opens the radio's setup network, joins it from your phone or computer, sets the page password and
saves your home-network details.

On return to Info, check that the radio has an address on your home network. The
[Info guide](../../components/avela_system_info/docs/user/INFO.md) explains those rows.

## Start listening

Press Back to reach the screen menu. Turn to Radio and press the knob. If the screen says Stopped, press the knob to
start. Turn the knob to choose a comfortable volume.

To choose something else, follow
[Choose a station](../../components/avela_radio/docs/user/RADIO.md#choose-a-station). The supplied station list
gives you a starting point; you can replace its entries with your own MP3 streams.

## Make it yours

Open the [setup page](../../components/avela_setup/docs/user/SETUP.md) from a device on your home network. Log in
with user name `avela-admin` and the page password you set during connection. From there you can edit the station
list, set your time zone and weather location, and change screen brightness. Each tab has its own Save or action
button.

For a quick reference during everyday use, keep [Controls](CONTROLS.md) to hand.
